Subscription Manager & Commitment Calendar

Finance

One place to manage every fixed recurring commitment — subscriptions, EMIs, insurance, rent — and a one-click flow to mark them paid so they automatically land in your expense log.

📋 The Manager Screen (Add / Edit / Remove)
  • You open a dedicated Subscriptions page from the Finance section. You see a clean list of all your active commitments — each card showing the name, icon, amount, and next due date.
  • Tap "+ Add Subscription" — a slide-up sheet appears with these fields: Name (e.g. Netflix), Amount, Renewal Frequency (Monthly / Quarterly / Yearly), Next Due Date, Category & Sub-Category (e.g. Entertainment → Streaming), Vendor / Brand (used across all reports), and a small Notes field.
  • Tap any existing subscription card → an Edit sheet opens pre-filled with the same fields. You can update or tap the red Remove button at the bottom to delete it.
  • The list auto-sorts by "Due Soonest" so you always see what's coming up first.
📅 The Calendar View (Commitment Forecast)
  • Switch to Calendar View — a month-grid lights up with due-date dots for each subscription. Hover or tap a date to see what renews.
  • A sticky "Committed This Month" banner at the top shows the total pre-committed spend, so you know before the month starts how much is already locked away.
  • Subscriptions that haven't been paid past their due date are highlighted in amber as "Zombie" — unused but still renewing.
✅ The Mark-as-Paid Flow (Renamed: "Log Payment")
  • Each subscription card has a "Log Payment" button. When the due date arrives, you tap it.
  • A quick confirm sheet appears pre-filled with: the Name, the Amount, the Category, Sub-Category, and Vendor — all pulled from what you set up. You can tweak any field before confirming.
  • Hit "Confirm & Add to Expenses" — the transaction is instantly added to your daily expense log with the correct category and vendor, and simultaneously posted to the Activity Timeline as a timestamped event.
  • The subscription card resets its due date to the next cycle automatically.
📊 Where this data shows up
📊 Finance Reports — by category/vendor
🕐 Activity Timeline — as paid event
📅 Calendar — as due-date dots
💡 Dashboard — committed spend banner
User value: You no longer miss renewal dates or wonder where your money went before the month even started. Every subscription payment is a real expense entry — tracked by category, sub-category, and vendor — so it feeds all your finance reports accurately.

Streak Freeze Joker Cards

Habits

Life happens — travel, sickness, a rough day. Joker Cards let you skip logging a habit without torching a long streak. A safety net, not a cheat code.

⚙️ How You Set It Up
  • Inside each habit's settings, a new "Streak Protection" toggle appears. Turn it on to activate Joker Cards for that habit.
  • Set how many Jokers you get per month (e.g. 2). This is your monthly allowance for guilt-free skips.
🃏 How You Use a Joker
  • On any day you missed logging a habit, the habit card shows a small 🃏 Use Joker option — visible until midnight or the next day.
  • Tap it → a quick confirmation appears: "Use 1 of your 2 Jokers for [Habit Name]?"
  • Confirm → the day is marked with a special joker icon instead of blank. Your streak is preserved. Your joker count ticks down.
  • When all Jokers are used up for the month, the option disappears. Streak risk is real again.
📊 Where it shows up
🎯 Habit Card — Joker icon on skipped days
📅 Heatmap — distinct color for Joker days
📊 Habit Report — Jokers used this month
User value: A 90-day streak on hydration shouldn't die because you had food poisoning on one day. Jokers keep momentum alive and make the system feel human.

Weekly & Custom Frequency Habits

Habits

Not every good habit needs to happen every single day. Set habits that recur on specific days or a set number of times per week — and log them without breaking a streak on off-days.

⚙️ Setting Up a Non-Daily Habit
  • When creating or editing a habit, a new "Frequency" option replaces the plain daily toggle. Options are: Every Day, Specific Days, or X Times Per Week.
  • Specific Days: A row of day-buttons (Mon–Sun) appears. Tap to select which days this habit is active. e.g. Gym only on Mon, Wed, Fri.
  • X Times Per Week: A stepper lets you pick a target count (e.g. 3). The app tracks how many times you've logged it in the current week vs. your target.
📱 How It Looks While Using
  • On days a habit is not scheduled, its card shows a "Rest Day" pill — greyed out, not checkable. Your streak is not at risk on rest days.
  • For "X times per week" habits, the card shows a mini progress bar: "2 / 3 this week" and counts down days left to hit the target.
  • A week is Mon–Sun. If you hit your target early (e.g. read on Mon, Tue, Wed when target is 3), the habit card shows a ✅ Weekly Goal Met banner for the rest of the week.
📊 Where it shows up
🏠 Dashboard — weekly progress mini-bar
📊 Habit Reports — weekly completion %
📅 Heatmap — rest days shown distinctly
User value: Going to the gym 3x a week is a perfectly healthy routine — the app should support it, not punish you with a broken streak on Thursday when you're supposed to rest.

Eisenhower Priority Matrix

Tasks

A 4-quadrant view for your tasks — organized by urgency and importance. Instead of a flat list, you see immediately what you must do today, what to plan, what to delegate, and what to drop.

📍 Where It Lives
  • In the Tasks section, a new "Matrix View" toggle appears alongside the existing list view — switch between them freely.
📱 The 4 Quadrants
  • Q1 — Do First (Urgent + Important): Highlighted in red. These demand action today. Deadlines, crises.
  • Q2 — Schedule (Not Urgent + Important): Highlighted in blue. Your growth work — planning, health, skills. Should dominate your week.
  • Q3 — Delegate (Urgent + Not Important): Highlighted in amber. Requests, errands — things that feel urgent but don't move the needle.
  • Q4 — Eliminate (Not Urgent + Not Important): Grey. Busywork and low-value activities.
⚙️ Tagging Tasks
  • When creating or editing a task, two quick toggles appear: Urgent? (Yes/No) and Important? (Yes/No). The app places it into the correct quadrant automatically.
  • Tasks with a due date within 48 hours are auto-flagged as Urgent unless you say otherwise.
  • You can drag tasks between quadrants directly on the matrix view to re-classify them.
📊 Where it shows up
📝 Tasks — Matrix View toggle
🏠 Dashboard — Q1 count badge
User value: Most people spend their days in Q1 and Q3 — fires and interruptions. The matrix makes it obvious when you're neglecting Q2 (your real growth), which is the single biggest thing successful people do differently.

Habit Stacking

Habits

Attach a task directly to a habit — so the moment you check off your morning run, a follow-up task (like "Drink protein shake") is automatically triggered and added to your task list.

⚙️ Setting Up a Stack
  • In a habit's settings, a new "Habit Stack" section appears. Tap "+ Add Linked Action".
  • A sheet appears where you type a short task description (e.g. "Journal for 5 min"), set its priority level, and optionally add a note. This becomes the auto-created task every time the habit is checked off.
  • You can stack multiple tasks to a single habit (e.g. After Workout → "Log macros" + "Record weight").
📱 When You Log the Habit
  • You tap Done on your Workout habit → a subtle toast notification slides in: "2 stacked tasks added to your list."
  • The Tasks section shows these auto-added items with a small 🔗 link icon indicating they were stacked from a habit.
  • Completing the stacked tasks also shows up in your activity timeline as linked events.
📊 Where it shows up
🎯 Habit Settings — Stack section
📝 Tasks — auto-added with 🔗 tag
🕐 Activity Timeline — linked events
User value: The science of habit stacking says attaching a new behaviour to an existing one is the most reliable way to make it stick. This feature turns that principle into a built-in system.

Unified "Life Velocity" Score (0–100)

Intelligence

A single number — 0 to 100 — that tells you how well you are living your life today, based on a weighted blend of your habits, tasks, budget, and workouts.

🧮 How the Score is Calculated
  • 50% — Habits: % of scheduled habits completed today vs. target.
  • 20% — Tasks: % of Q1 and Q2 tasks completed this week.
  • 20% — Budget: How close actual spending is to planned budget (within target = full 20).
  • 10% — Workout: Sessions completed vs. your weekly goal.
📱 How It Looks
  • On the Dashboard, a large radial gauge (like a speedometer) fills with your current score. Color shifts: red (0–40), amber (41–70), green (71–100).
  • Below the gauge: a small breakdown showing your score contribution from each pillar — so you instantly see which area is dragging your number down.
  • A 30-day trend sparkline below that shows if your Life Velocity is rising or falling over the past month.
⚡ How is Feature 20 Different from Feature 15 (Life RPG)?
  • Feature 15 (Life RPG) is a motivational reward system — it gamifies your actions to make you want to do them. It's about fun, dopamine, and streaks. XP goes up; you buy rewards; you fight boss battles. It's forward-looking and playful.
  • Feature 20 (Life Velocity) is a diagnostic measurement tool — it tells you objectively how well-balanced your life is right now. It has no rewards, no XP. It's a score that goes up when you're balanced and drops when you neglect an area. It's analytical and honest.
  • Together: RPG makes you want to take action → Life Velocity tells you if the actions you're taking are the right ones in the right proportions.
📊 Where it shows up

📊 Reports — 30-day trend chart
📅 Weekly Review — score comparison
User value: On a bad week you might hit your workouts but blow your budget and skip your habits. Without a unified score, you feel fine because one metric is green. Life Velocity catches the imbalance and puts a number on it.

26

Offline Mode & Background Sync

System

The app works fully even without internet — you can log habits, add expenses, and track workouts in the gym basement or on a flight. Everything syncs silently to the cloud the moment you're back online.

📱 The Offline Experience
  • When you lose connectivity, a small "Offline" indicator badge appears in the header — subtle, not alarming. The app continues to function normally.
  • All actions you take offline are saved locally in the browser's storage (IndexedDB). You can log habits, add expenses, update tasks, and log sets — all without internet.
  • Locally cached data means previously loaded pages (today's dashboard, your habit list, recent expenses) are available even with zero connectivity.
🔄 Syncing Back Online
  • The moment the internet returns, a Service Worker running in the background detects the connection and automatically pushes all queued offline actions to the Supabase database — in the order they were made.
  • A brief "Synced ✓" toast confirms everything is saved. No manual action needed.
  • If there's ever a conflict (e.g. you edited something on two devices while offline), the app shows a simple merge prompt asking which version to keep.
🔔 Push Notifications (Bonus, Part of This Feature)
  • When you grant notification permission, the app can send daily reminders (e.g. "You haven't logged your habits today"), subscription renewal alerts (e.g. "Netflix renews tomorrow — ₹649"), and workout reminders for planned sessions.
  • All notification schedules are configurable per-item and in a central Notification Settings panel.
📊 Where it shows up
📶 Header — Offline status badge
🔄 Background — auto-sync on reconnect
🔔 System — push notification centre
⚙️ Settings — Notification preferences
User value: A life-tracking app that requires internet is broken by design. The gym has spotty WiFi. The plane has none. Offline mode means the app is always with you — not conditionally with you.

💰 Finance & Budget 1 Feature

Finance 1.1 Savings

Savings Goals Tracker

Define specific financial savings targets like "Save ₹5L for trip" and monitor progress dynamically against your total liquidity.

  • Track progress dynamically against liquid accounts balances.
  • Visual progress percentages with projection timelines of when you'll reach targets.
📋 Target User Flow
  • Tap "+ New Goal" in your dashboard finance card.
  • Enter Goal Name, Target Amount, Target Date, and select contributing liquidity accounts.
  • A progress bar updates balances dynamically and calculates savings velocity.
